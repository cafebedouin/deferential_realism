# Philosophical Foundations

This directory contains the core philosophical architecture of Deferential Realism—the theoretical grounding for constraint classification and indexed epistemology.

## Reading Order

1. **Start with [`../core.md`](../core.md)** - Compressed introduction (required first)
2. **[epistemology.md](epistemology.md)** - How to classify constraints accurately
3. **[logic.md](logic.md)** - Formal operators for constraint reasoning
4. **[metaphysics.md](metaphysics.md)** - What constraints are made of

## Documents

### [epistemology.md](epistemology.md) (~7,000 words)
**How to Classify Constraints Accurately Under Uncertainty**

The central document for understanding DR's approach to knowledge:
- Intellectual lineage (Stoicism → Existentialism → Pragmatism → Systems Theory)
- Four-category constraint ontology (Mountain, Rope, Noose, Scaffold)
- Classification methodology:
  - Six-Test Battery
  - Hybrid Decomposition Protocol
  - Language vs. Function Audit
- Honest limitations and epistemic positioning

### [logic.md](logic.md) (~4,000 words)
**Formal Operators for Indexed Constraint Reasoning**

The formal machinery for reasoning about constraints:
- Index structure: `I ::= (Power, Time, Exit, Scope)`
- Modal operators: `■C[I]` (Mountain), `⊞C[I]` (Rope), `⊠C[I]` (Noose), `⊞⊠C[I]` (Tangled)
- Inference rules and transformations
- Contradiction resolution through indexing
- Executable specification in Prolog

### [metaphysics.md](metaphysics.md) (~5,600 words)
**Constraint-Space Ontology**

What exists and how constraints define identity:
- Core claim: constraints constitute entities, not vice versa
- Structural realism over substance metaphysics
- Why DR adopts constraint-first ontology
- Alternative ontologies and trade-offs

### [ethics.md](ethics.md) (~3,700 words)
**Four Cardinal Virtues of Constraint-Alignment**

What to do once you've classified constraints:
- **Acceptance** - Mapping Mountains accurately
- **Resistance** - Cutting Nooses strategically
- **Creation** - Building functional Ropes
- **Conservation** - Preserving finite energy
- Reality-alignment as honesty-in-practice

### [aesthetics.md](aesthetics.md) (~4,400 words)
**Beauty of Alignment vs. Ugliness of Fraud**

The aesthetic dimension of constraint navigation:
- Elegant surrender to Mountains (beauty)
- Grotesque forced compliance to Nooses (ugliness)
- Well-designed Ropes that disappear into infrastructure
- The aesthetics of ontological fraud (disguising Nooses as Mountains)
