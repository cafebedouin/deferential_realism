# Daily Practice: Constraint Navigation Protocols

This document provides the complete daily practice system for Psychological Deferential Realism.

## Overview

P-DR is not a theory—it's a daily practice of:
1. **Morning:** Classify what constrains you today
2. **During day:** Navigate based on classification
3. **Evening:** Review accuracy, update calibration

**Time required:**
- Morning: 10 minutes
- During day: Ongoing awareness
- Evening: 5 minutes
- Weekly: 15 minutes

## Morning Navigation Diagnostic (10 minutes)

**Purpose:** Start the day with clear constraint classification

**Before the day's surrogate activities cloud your vision, run this protocol.**

### 1. The Substrate Scan (2 minutes)

**Identify one biological or structural limit you are tempted to fight today.**

**The Question:**
> "What is the Mountain I am currently pretending is a choice?"

**Common examples:**
- "I can push through on 4 hours sleep" (when you need 7-8)
- "I should be able to focus in chaos" (when your nervous system needs quiet)
- "I ought to be comfortable with this" (when it violates your temperament)
- "I can skip meals today" (when you're already running on empty)

**The Action:** Defer. Structure your schedule around it rather than against it.

**Recording (use worksheet):**
```
Mountain identified: _______________________
How I've been fighting it: _______________________
How I'll defer today: _______________________
```

### 2. The Edge Detection (3 minutes)

**Identify one "I can't" or "I'm not" that feels like a tomb you've outgrown.**

**The Question:**
> "Where am I predicting damage but only feeling discomfort?"

**Common examples:**
- "I can't speak up in meetings" (but haven't tried in 5 years)
- "I'm not a morning person" (but never tested it systematically)
- "I can't handle conflict" (but predicting catastrophe without data)
- "I'm not creative" (from one discouraging comment years ago)

**The Action:** Plan one Edge Test today. Violate the constraint slightly and observe if the world breaks or if only your ego winces.

**Recording:**
```
Detritus claim: _______________________
When it formed: _______________________
Edge test I'll run today: _______________________
Prediction: _______________________
(Fill in observation after test)
```

### 3. The Demand Check (2 minutes)

**Notice where you are refusing to move until you "know for sure."**

**The Question:**
> "Am I demanding certainty to avoid the vulnerability of action?"

**Common examples:**
- "I need to know if this relationship will work before I commit"
- "I can't start until I understand the whole system"
- "I have to research more before deciding" (already on month 10)
- "I need clarity about my purpose before I act"

**The Action:** Drop the demand. Commit to Inquiry through Motion. Act as if uncertainty is permanent.

**Recording:**
```
Certainty demand: _______________________
What I'm avoiding by demanding it: _______________________
First action I'll take without knowing: _______________________
```

### 4. The Membrane Check (3 minutes)

**Scan your upcoming interactions (Ropes and Nooses).**

**The Question:**
> "Who is trying to reclassify my Mountains as their Ropes?"

**Common examples:**
- Someone calling your sleep need "unreasonable"
- Someone treating your boundary as "being difficult"
- Someone reframing their extraction as "mutual obligation"
- Someone demanding you violate substrate for their convenience

**The Action:** Recalibrate permeability. Set your membrane to:
- **Impermeable** for Nooses
- **Selective** for Ropes
- **High permeability** for Mountains

**Recording:**
```
Interaction today: _______________________
Their classification: _______________________
My classification: _______________________
Membrane setting: High permeability / Selective / Impermeable
What I'll say if they push: _______________________
```

## During Day: The Calibration Table

**Use this for rapid classification when you feel resistance:**

| **If it feels like...** | **It is likely...** | **Your Move:** |
|------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Inevitable Weight | Substrate (Mountain) | Defer. Navigate around it. |
| Old Ghost/Shame | Residue (Detritus) | Test. Violate the pattern. |
| Analysis Paralysis | Clarity Fetish | Drop. Act without knowing. |
| Structural Tension | Paradox | Hold. Refuse to solve it. |
| Someone demanding compliance | Potential Noose | Check: Mountain, Rope, Noose? |
| Discomfort but no damage | Edge of Detritus | Push slightly further. |
| Actual harm occurring | Substrate violation | Stop immediately. This is real. |

**The Practice:**
1. **Notice resistance**
2. **Classify using table**
3. **Navigate accordingly**
4. **Note result for evening review**

**Purpose:** Update calibration based on day's data

### The Three Questions

**1. Classification Check**
```

After testing, it was actually _____________.
What this teaches me: _____________.
Update my calibration: _____________.
```

**2. Edge Test Results**
```
I tested: _____________
Predicted: _____________
Observed: _____________
Actual constraint type: _____________
What I learned: _____________
```

**3. Membrane Accuracy**
```
I set permeability to _____________ for _____________.
Result: [Held well / Needs adjustment]
What happened: _____________
Adjustment needed: _____________
```

### Quick Logging

**Don't write essays. Just note:**
- What you classified
- What happened
- What you learned
- What you'll adjust

**Example:**
```
Classification: Thought "public speaking" was Mountain
Test: Spoke in small meeting
Result: Just discomfort, no damage → Detritus
Update: Test larger audience next
```

## The Litany of the Real (Threshold Moments)

**When to use:**
- Morning (before beginning day)
- Before difficult conversations
- When feeling stuck
- At transitions

**The words:**
```
I shall not feign freedom where constraints bind.
Constraint is the structure of the world, not its end.

I will permit the Mountains to pass through me.
Where they have passed, I will remain.

I will turn my inner eye to the Detritus.
Where the Detritus was, I will test the edge.

Only the Substrate will persist.

I will hold the Paradox without collapse.
I will say Yes to the navigation.
```

**Purpose:** Shift from contemplation to calibration. From thinking about patterns to preparing to test them.

## The Molly Bloom Protocol (When Fragmented)

**Use when:** The day becomes fragmented and the Ship of Theseus feels like it's falling apart.

**The Practice:**

1. **Notice the fragmentation**
   - "The ship is falling apart" feeling
   - Multiple constraints pulling in different directions
   - Sense of being overwhelmed by complexity

2. **Name the frames you're seeing**
   - **Material:** What's actually changing/aging/breaking
   - **Functional:** What continues despite changes
   - **Historical:** The context that won't shift

3. **Say yes to being in all of it**
   - Not yes to liking it
   - Not yes to it being resolved
   - Yes to navigating it anyway

**The words:**
> "Yes to the constraint. Yes to the navigation. Yes to being in the ship while it's rebuilt."

**What this does:** Returns you to containment stance. Drops the demand for resolution. Accepts navigation as ongoing.

## Crisis Navigation Protocol (Emergency)

**Use when:** Everything feels like it's breaking, you're overwhelmed, can't think clearly.

**The Emergency Sequence:**

**1. STOP**
- Literally pause movement/speech
- Three deep breaths
- Remember: You're in the ship while it's being rebuilt

**2. SUBSTRATE CHECK**
- "What Mountains am I violating right now?"
- Sleep? Food? Safety? Actual capacity limits?
- **If violating substrate: DEFER IMMEDIATELY**

**3. ATTRIBUTION TEST**
- "Is this constraint internal or external?"
- "Am I observing or predicting?"
- "Have I actually tested this?"

**4. FRACTURE SCAN**
- F01: Am I forcing certainty I don't have?
- F07: Am I demanding certainty before acting?
- F13: Am I analyzing instead of engaging?
- F19: Am I forcing resolution of paradox?
- F22: Am I mislocating the constraint?

**5. MINIMUM VIABLE NAVIGATION**
- "What's the smallest next move?"
- "What can I defer to right now?"
- "What edge can I test with minimal stakes?"
- "What certainty demand can I drop immediately?"

**6. RETURN TO LITANY**
```
I shall not feign freedom where constraints bind.
I will permit the Mountains to pass through me.
I will hold the Paradox without collapse.
I will say Yes to the navigation.
```

## Common Practice Mistakes

### 1. Over-Classification (F13: Simulated Depth)

**What it looks like:** Spending hours analyzing constraint types instead of testing them.

**The trap:** Using classification as sophisticated avoidance of substrate contact.

**The fix:** If you've spent >10 minutes classifying, STOP. Test instead.

### 2. Certainty About Classification (F01: Forced Certainty)

**What it looks like:** "I'm SURE this is substrate" without testing.

**The trap:** Demanding certainty about classification (meta-clarity fetish).

**The fix:** Hold classification tentatively. Be wrong. Update.

### 3. Pushing Past Damage (Substrate Violation)

**What it looks like:** "I'll push through this pain/exhaustion."

**The trap:** Mistaking substrate for detritus, causing actual harm.

**The fix:** STOP at damage signals. Err on side of substrate.

### 4. Never Testing (Treating All Detritus as Mountains)

**What it looks like:** "I can't" without ever trying.

**The trap:** Living in empty tombs, never discovering they're empty.

**The fix:** Test systematically. Most "I can't" is detritus.

### 5. Forcing Resolution (F19: Containment Breach)

**What it looks like:** "I MUST choose between autonomy OR connection."

**The trap:** Demanding paradoxes resolve, collapsing into false simplicity.

**The fix:** Hold both. Choose frame per context. Accept partial satisfaction.

## Progress Indicators

**You're practicing well when:**

1. **Classification speed increases** (recognize patterns faster)
2. **Substrate violations decrease** (you defer before damage)
3. **Detritus clearing happens** (empty tombs released)
4. **Clarity fetishes dissolve** (you act without knowing)
5. **Paradoxes held without strain** (containment becomes natural)
6. **Membrane accuracy improves** (boundaries work)
7. **You catch fractures early** (F01-F36 recognition)

**The goal is NOT:**
- Zero constraints
- Perfect clarity
- All paradoxes resolved
- Complete self-knowledge

**The goal IS:**
- Accurate classification
- Honest navigation
- Fewer substrate violations
- More detritus cleared
- Better paradox containment

## Integration with Life

**Relationships:** Use membrane check daily. Test attributions. Hold autonomy/connection paradox.

**Work:** Identify substrate (your actual capacity limits). Clear detritus (old "I can't" patterns). Drop clarity fetishes (act without perfect information).

**Creativity:** Test edge of "good enough." Drop certainty demands. Defer to substrate (actual skill limits for now).

**Community:** Run externalist diagnostics (is this them or me?). Test fit (substrate incompatibility or fear?). Manage membrane (what crosses boundary?).

## The Practice Never Ends

**From the foundations document:**

> "You are in the ship while it's being rebuilt. External constraints operate. Internal constraints operate. Clarity doesn't eliminate them.
>
> You classify them. You test them. You navigate them. You choose your frame. You accept what remains unresolved.
>
> And somewhere in all of that, you find your yes."

**This is the practice:**
- Morning: Classify
- Day: Navigate
- Evening: Update
- Weekly: Map
- Crisis: Return to basics
- Always: Test, don't assume
