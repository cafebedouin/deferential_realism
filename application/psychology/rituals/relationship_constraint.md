# Example: Relationship Constraint

**Situation:** "I can't commit to this relationship"

This worked example shows the complete P-DR protocol applied to a common relationship constraint.

## Step 1: Notice & Articulate

**The claim:** "I can't commit to this relationship"

**Initial feeling:** Anxiety, sense of being trapped, uncertainty about "rightness"

**First articulation:**
"I feel like I can't fully commit to this relationship. Something feels off but I can't name it. Every time I think about moving forward (moving in together, meeting family, future planning), I feel anxious and want to pull back."

## Step 2: Classify the Constraint

**Question:** Is this Mountain, Detritus, or Clarity Fetish?

### Testing for Mountain (Substrate)

**Mountain indicators would be:**
- Fundamental value misalignment (kids/no kids, monogamy/polyamory)
- Actual temperament incompatibility (extreme introvert with extreme extrovert needing opposite things)
- Life direction incompatibility (wants city, you need wilderness for mental health)

**Check:**
- Values: Aligned on major life questions (checked)
- Temperament: Both introverts, similar energy patterns (checked)
- Life direction: Both want similar lifestyle (checked)

**Result:** No clear Mountain indicators. Move to next test.

### Testing for Detritus (Piton Constraint)

**Detritus indicators would be:**
- Pattern repeats across multiple relationships
- "I'm just not feeling it" has been said before
- "The timing is wrong" every time
- Fear-based prediction without testing

**Check:**
- Is this pattern familiar? YES - every relationship at this stage, I pull back
- When did this pattern form? After painful breakup at 23 (now 32)
- What was the original function? Protect from being hurt again
- Does that circumstance still apply? No - different person, different context
- Have I tested commitment since then? No - always exit before this point

**Investigation reveals:**
Original context: Partner cheated after I "opened up completely"
Cached response: "If I don't commit fully, I can't be hurt fully"
Current reality: Different person, 9 years later, no signs of that pattern

**Result:** Strong Detritus indicators. But check Clarity Fetish before classifying.

### Testing for Clarity Fetish

**Clarity Fetish indicators would be:**
- "I need to know for sure this will work before committing"
- Demanding guarantee before taking vulnerability
- Certainty demand blocks the data-gathering that would reduce uncertainty

**Check:**
- Am I demanding certainty? YES - "I need to know this is The One before I commit"
- Does the demand block knowing? YES - can't know without committing
- What am I avoiding? Vulnerability of not knowing outcome
- Is this a demand or a limit? Demand (I could act without knowing, choosing not to)

**Result:** BOTH Detritus AND Clarity Fetish operating simultaneously.

**Classification:**
- **Primary:** Detritus (historical pattern from old wound)
- **Secondary:** Clarity Fetish (using "need to know" to justify avoiding)

## Step 3: Run Externalist Diagnostic

**Attribution Test:** Is this constraint internal or external?

**Check:**
- Have I actually engaged deeply enough to know? MIXED - engaged emotionally but blocked practical commitment
- Does this pattern appear across all relationships? YES - last 3 relationships, same point, same pullback
- Is friction unique to this person? NO - same pattern regardless of partner quality

**Result:** Pattern suggests INTERNAL constraint (detritus), not substrate incompatibility with this person.

**Environmental Test:** Am I using available resources?

**Check:**
- Have I talked to partner about fears? NO
- Have I sought couple's counseling? NO
- Have I tested small commitments? NO - all-or-nothing thinking

**Result:** Haven't tested external resources. Assuming constraint operates alone.

## Step 4: Check for Fractures

**Scanning F01-F36:**

**F01 (Forced Certainty):** Are you claiming to know something you don't?
- "I know this won't work" ← Not actually tested

**F07 (Premature Certainty Demand):** Are you requiring resolution before inquiry?
- "I need to know if this is forever before I commit" ← Classic F07
- Can't know "forever" without time; demand blocks time

**F13 (Simulated Depth):** Using analysis to avoid engagement?
- Spending hours analyzing "is this right?" instead of testing small commitments
- Sophistication as avoidance

**F22 (Attribution Inversion):** Mislocating the constraint source?
- Treating internal fear (detritus) as relationship incompatibility (external substrate)
- YES - attributing internal pattern to external mismatch

**Result:** Multiple fractures active. F07 and F22 most prominent.

## Step 5: Apply Appropriate Protocol

**Classification Summary:**
- **Detritus** (primary): Old protection mechanism from past hurt
- **Clarity Fetish** (secondary): Using certainty demand to justify avoidance
- **Fractures:** F07, F22 active

**Intervention Strategy:**

### For Detritus Component

**Protocol:** Investigate → Test → Clear

**Investigate:**
```
When formed: After betrayal at 23
Function then: Prevent deep hurt by preventing deep commitment
Function now: Blocks intimacy without serving protection (partner isn't betrayer)
Context shift: 9 years, therapy, different person, different patterns
```

**Test edges:**
"What happens if I commit slightly?"

**Edge Test Design:**
```
Small commitment test: Agree to meet partner's family (weekend trip)
Prediction: Will feel trapped, will want to run
Safety bounds: Can leave early if actual panic (not just discomfort)
Observation plan: Note difference between discomfort vs. damage
```

### For Clarity Fetish Component

**Protocol:** Drop → Act → Notice

**Drop the demand:**
"I accept I cannot know if this relationship is 'forever' before living into it."

**Act without knowing:**
"I will make next-level commitment (meeting family) without guarantee of outcome."

**Notice when it resurfaces:**
"When the voice says 'but you need to know first,' recognize it as F07 and proceed anyway."

### For Attribution Inversion (F22)

**Protocol:** Test attribution

**Current attribution:** "The relationship isn't right"
**Alternative attribution:** "My fear pattern from 9 years ago is operating"

**Test:** 
- Track: Does discomfort increase with commitment actions or with partner's behavior?
- If discomfort increases when partner is most trustworthy → internal pattern
- If discomfort increases with actual red flags → external substrate

## Step 6: Execute & Document

### Week 1: Edge Test

**Action:** Agreed to family weekend trip

**Prediction:** Would feel trapped, want to run

**Observation:**
- Day 1: High anxiety (expected)
- Day 2: Anxiety decreased (interesting - just discomfort?)
- Day 3: Actually enjoyed it, anxiety minimal
- Post-trip: Relief, not trapped feeling

**Result:** Discomfort only, not damage. Detritus confirmed.

**Partner behavior check:** Was consistently kind, didn't pressure, respected boundaries
**Internal pattern check:** Anxiety highest BEFORE trip, decreased during (pattern-based, not reality-based)

### Week 2: Slightly Larger Commitment

**Action:** Discussed moving in together timeline (6 months out)

**Prediction:** Would panic and want to exit relationship

**Observation:**
- Initial anxiety spike (familiar pattern)
- Used Molly Bloom protocol: "Yes to being in uncertainty"
- Dropped certainty demand: "I don't need to know it's forever"
- Discomfort present but manageable
- No damage, no desire to exit

**Result:** Pattern loosening. Clarity fetish dissolving when dropped.

### Week 4: Review

**Original classification:** Detritus + Clarity Fetish
**Confirmed:** YES - testing shows no substrate incompatibility
**Progress:** Pattern clearing, demand dropping

**Cleared detritus:** Protection mechanism from 23 (tomb is now empty)
**Dropped clarity fetish:** Can commit without knowing "forever"
**New substrate discovered:** Need for slower pacing (actual temperament, not fear)

## Step 7: Ongoing Navigation

### Mountains Discovered (Defer To)

**Actual substrate:**
"I need slower relationship pacing than typical - my processing speed for emotional decisions"

**How I'll defer:**
- Communicate this need explicitly to partner
- Structure commitment increases around my actual pace (not fear-based pace)
- Accept this as temperament, not defect

### Cleared Detritus

**Empty tomb recognized:**
"The betrayal at 23 taught me something valuable then (be cautious). But the lesson ossified into unnecessary limitation. The tomb is empty - I'm not 23, this isn't that person, I have more capacity now."

### Dropped Clarity Fetish

**Demand released:**
"I wanted to know 'is this forever' before committing. But I can only know through committing. The demand was the block. Drop demand, gain data."

### Paradox Held

**Autonomy vs. Connection:**
- Need for independence (real)
- Need for deep connection (real)
- Can't maximize both (structural)
- Choose emphasis per context: Solo time honored, deep time honored, calibrate regularly

## Outcome

**6 Months Later:**

**Moved in together.** Anxiety appeared (old pattern) but was recognized as detritus, not signal. Tested it (committed anyway), observed (discomfort only, no damage).

**Relationship deepened.** Not because problems went away, but because I stopped treating fear as data about the relationship.

**Substrate respect improved.** Discovered I need more alone time than average partner - this IS substrate (real need). Partner accommodates, I don't apologize for it.

**Clarity fetish gone.** Comfortable acting without knowing "forever." Get data through living, not through analysis.

**Paradox held without collapse.** Autonomy and connection both honored. Some days emphasize one, some the other. Don't demand resolution.

## Key Lessons

### 1. Internal Patterns Can Feel Like External Reality

My fear felt like "this relationship isn't right." Testing revealed it was "my old pattern activating." Attribution inversion (F22) is subtle.

### 2. Multiple Constraint Types Can Operate Simultaneously

Detritus (old wound) AND Clarity Fetish (demand for certainty) AND later-discovered Substrate (actual pacing need). Classification isn't always singular.

### 3. Testing Reveals Truth, Analysis Obscures It

I spent months analyzing "is this right?" Testing (committing slightly, observing) gave data in weeks.

### 4. Substrate Emerges Through Testing

Didn't know my "need for slower pacing" was substrate until I cleared the detritus and could feel the real signal underneath.

### 5. Cleared Detritus Doesn't Mean No Limits

Clearing the fear-based pattern revealed actual limits (pacing need). But these are navigable substrate, not piton constraints.

## For Your Own Practice

When facing "I can't commit to this relationship":

1. **Don't assume it's substrate** (incompatibility) or detritus (fear) initially
2. **Run the classification protocol** systematically
3. **Check for fractures** (especially F07, F22)
4. **Test edges** - commit slightly, observe
5. **Distinguish discomfort from damage**
6. **Update classification** as data comes in
7. **Respect substrate when found** (real incompatibility exists)
8. **Clear detritus when found** (most "I can't" is clearable)

**The goal isn't forcing yourself to commit. It's knowing WHY you can't—substrate (real incompatibility to defer to) or detritus (clearable pattern).**
