**Yuki Afterword – 15 December 2182**  
I returned to the apartment three days after the lab called. They said he'd been found slumped at his bench, vials scattered, notebook open. No pulse at first, then faint. Hospital now—induced coma, neural scans inconclusive. "Remodeling," they said. "Irreversible."  

I went through the study. The drawer was unlocked this time. Empty vials, faint residue glowing blue under blacklight.

I tested one—trace phenethylamine derivative, unknown impurity. No match in databases.  

His parents asked what happened. I said work stress, that he pushed too hard. They nodded, but his father stared at the locked lab reports I wouldn't share.  

Chen messaged: "If there's anything..." I didn't reply.  

The notebook ends mid-sentence. "Attempting manual override via—" Via what? More chemistry? Himself?  

I keep rereading the entries. The bifurcation he noted—Ken, efficient, necessary. Was that him, or the thing that replaced him?  

The apartment feels bifurcated now. His futon still laid out, untouched. The kitchen light I left on for him.  

I stand in the study sometimes, hand on the drawer knob. If I open it again, what lies inside? The truth, or just more blue glow fading?  

I close it. Listen to the wind outside. Wait for him to come home, knowing he won't. Not as himself. Not as anyone.  
What was the override? What did he attempt?  

The questions loop. The blue waits. Faint, but persistent.
