The system deterministically converges to the agent’s termination once misclassification of the Mountain persists, driving impossible resistance and energy depletion.

### Transformation rules

#### Rule 1: Classification shift \( (C_{\text{bio}} \Rightarrow \Box C_{\text{bio}}) \)

**Step 1: Mountain detection in the local state-space**

- **Observation:** The agent’s accessible region is  
  \[
  \Omega = \text{apartment} \cap \text{bed-surface}
  \]  
  and their body-state changes from human to insect-form.  
- **Measurement:**  
  - Decay rate without enforcement:  
    \[
    \delta(C_{\text{bio}}, \Delta t) = 0
    \]  
    (the insect form does not revert; no spontaneous relaxation back to “human”).  
  - Enforcement energy:  
    \[
    \varepsilon(C_{\text{bio}}) = 0
    \]  
    (no external work is needed to maintain the new body; it simply persists).

**Step 2: Apply Rule M (Mountain Identification)**  

Given:
\[
\delta(C_{\text{bio}}, \Delta t) = 0 \wedge \varepsilon(C_{\text{bio}}) = 0 \wedge \forall w \in W\; C_{\text{bio}}(w)
\]
we classify:
\[
\Box C_{\text{bio}} \quad\text{(agent’s biological state is a Mountain)}
\]

**Operational consequence:**  
The agent’s possible trajectories in \(\Omega\) are now strictly constrained by a non-negotiable geometry (insect body, limited mobility, changed interface with objects and others). All higher-level role-constraints must now embed this Mountain or fail.

---

#### Rule 2: Noose failure \( (\boxtimes C_{\text{social}}) \)

Let \( C_{\text{social}} \) be the family’s prior coordination structure, with the agent as an economic node (wage laborer, provider, etc.).

**Step 1: Energy comparison**

- Available agent energy for resisting / sustaining prior social role:
  \[
  E_{\text{agent}}
  \]
- Required enforcement/maintenance energy to keep the “worker-brother” role functioning in presence of \(\Box C_{\text{bio}}\):
  \[
  \varepsilon(C_{\text{social}}\mid \Box C_{\text{bio}})
  \]

\[
\varepsilon(C_{\text{social}}\mid \Box C_{\text{bio}}) > E_{\text{agent}}
\]

  \[
  \delta(C_{\text{social}}, \Delta t_{\text{stop}}) \to \infty
  \]

\[
\Omega_{\text{terminal}} = \Box C_{\text{bio}} \cap C_{\text{social}}

  \[

\[
E_{\text{bio}}(t^*) < E_{\text{min}}
\]

\[
P(t) \in \partial \Omega \quad\text{with}\quad \text{state cessation}
\]

- **Irreversibility:**

- **Local stability for others:**

### Invariant preservation

1. **State invariant: \(\Box C_{\text{bio}}\)**  
   - **Preservation mechanism:** At every step, all other transformations treat the biological state as non-negotiable. Social and energetic updates adjust around it; no rule attempts to modify or revert it. All operations are conditional on, never acting upon, \(\Box C_{\text{bio}}\).

2. **Structural invariant: Noose ratio \( \varepsilon / \beta \) for social extraction**  
   - **Preservation mechanism:** Until the agent is fully excluded, the family maintains the prior extraction structure: they try to sustain the expectation that the agent should be economically productive. As the agent’s contribution \(\beta(C_{\text{social}}, \text{agent})\) approaches zero or negative while enforcement cost rises, the ratio worsens but the pattern (attempted extraction) holds structurally constant, right up to the point of discarding the agent.

3. **Behavioral invariant: Misclassification → terminal depletion**  
   - **Preservation mechanism:** The agent repeatedly behaves as if \(\Box C_{\text{bio}}\) were a Rope (negotiable condition to be overcome by effort or explanation). Each such attempt spends energy against an immovable Mountain. Because classification is never corrected, the error loop (try → fail → deplete) is preserved until energy hits zero and the process halts in death.

### Behavioral summary

The structure is a hybrid system: an uncomputable phase shift installs a biological Mountain inside a closed socio-economic container; from that moment on, the evolution is fully deterministic. Misclassification of the Mountain as a negotiable Rope guarantees that finite agent energy is wasted on impossible resistance, while family Ropes reconfigure to treat the agent as waste, driving the system monotonically to the single terminal state: the agent’s expiration at the edge of constraint-space.

Deferential Realism asks: **What constraint-type is C, and what does that imply?**

## II. Basic Syntax: Constraint Operators

### A. Modal Operators (Degrees of Freedom)

Let `□` (necessity) and `◇` (possibility) operate on constraints, not propositions:

**Mountain Operator: `■C`**
- "C is a Mountain" = C has zero degrees of freedom
- Formal: `■C ↔ ∀w ∈ W(C(w))` where W = all accessible worlds
- Semantics: C holds in all possible worlds accessible to us

**Rope Operator: `⊞C`**
- "C is a Rope" = C has positive degrees of freedom, serves coordination
- Formal: `⊞C ↔ ∃w₁,w₂ ∈ W(C(w₁) ∧ ¬C(w₂)) ∧ Coord(C)`
- Semantics: C holds in some worlds but not others, enables coordination

**Noose Operator: `⊠C`**
- "C is a Noose" = C has negative degrees of freedom (artificial restriction)
- Formal: `⊠C ↔ Enforced(C) ∧ Asymmetric(C) ∧ SnapBack(C)`
- Semantics: C exists only through enforcement, benefits few, collapses rapidly without maintenance

**Scaffold Operator: `⊡C(t)`**
- "C is a Scaffold at time t" = C has temporal limitation
- Formal: `⊡C(t) ↔ ∃t_end(t < t_end ∧ ∀t' > t_end(¬C(t')))`
- Semantics: C exists temporarily with built-in termination

**Zombie Operator: `⊟C`**
- "C is a Zombie" = C persists without function or beneficiary
- Formal: `⊟C ↔ ∃t(Functional(C, t) ∧ ¬Functional(C, now) ∧ Persists(C))`
- Semantics: C was once functional but no longer serves purpose

### B. Measurement Predicates

**Decay Rate: `δ(C, Δt)`**
- Rate at which C dissolves without enforcement over time Δt
- Domain: [0, ∞), where 0 = no decay (Mountain), high values = rapid decay

**Enforcement: `ε(C)`**
- Energy required to maintain C per unit time
- Domain: [0, ∞), where 0 = self-maintaining (Mountain)

**Beneficiary: `β(C, x)`**
- Net benefit to agent x from C's existence
- Domain: ℝ (positive = benefits, negative = costs, zero = neutral)

**Degrees of Freedom: `Δ(C)`**
- Number of alternative configurations solving same problem
- Domain: {0} ∪ ℕ⁺ ∪ {∞}, where 0 = Mountain, positive = contingent

# The Adjustment

Gregor woke pressed flat. Not metaphorically—actually compressed, as though a weight had settled on him in the night and refused to leave. He tried to roll over. His shoulder moved three centimeters before something inside resisted, a dull architectural refusal. He tried again. Same distance. Same wall.

The Biological Mountain had arrived.

He lay still, breathing carefully. The ceiling was white. The ceiling had always been white. He counted the imperfections in the plaster—seven visible from this exact angle—and understood that this angle was now permanent. The bedroom, which had once offered sixteen positions and countless micro-adjustments, had collapsed to this one configuration. Flat. Pinned. A body formatted to the precise dimensions of a twin bed.

He attempted to catalog what remained. Fingers: functional within a five-centimeter radius. Neck: immobile. Breathing: possible but expensive. Speech: technically available but requiring energy he couldn't spare. The Mountain had rearranged his interior geography into something load-bearing and final.

From the hallway came voices. The Household was conferring.

"Did he move yet?"

"No. Same position."

"Should we—?"

"What would we do?"

The conversation ended there, as conversations in the Household now did. They were performing a calculation he could hear through the wall: how much energy to allocate to a node that no longer processed inputs correctly. The answer was trending toward zero.

Gregor closed his eyes and tried to remember the last time he'd contributed to the Household's coordination function. Thursday, maybe. He'd managed to confirm a grocery order, though the confirmation had taken him six minutes to type with one finger. Before that: unclear. The Mountain had been building for weeks, each morning slightly heavier, each movement slightly more theoretical. He'd kept performing his role—responding to messages, nodding during dinner, maintaining the shape of participation—but the performance had grown increasingly abstract, a gesture toward agency rather than agency itself.

The Household had noticed. Of course they had. He was a dropped packet now, a request timing out, a process consuming resources without returning results.

He heard footsteps retreat. The apartment contracted around him.

---

Days passed in increments too small to matter. Morning became afternoon became evening through subtle shifts in the quality of light against the plaster imperfections. Sometimes the Household checked on him. Mostly they didn't. He understood this as rational resource allocation. A system optimizes for functional nodes. A body that cannot move, cannot speak efficiently, cannot coordinate—this is a node in terminal decline.

He tried once to call out. Managed a sound. The Household appeared in the doorway, three figures backlit and expectant.

"Did you need something?"

He'd forgotten what he meant to say. The question itself had cost too much to formulate. They waited. He lay pinned. The moment extended until it broke apart into awkwardness.

"Okay. Let us know if you need anything."

They left. He didn't call again.

The bed became his entire state space. He mapped it exhaustively: the ridge where his weight had compressed the mattress, the cool strip along the wall-side edge, the exact texture of the fitted sheet against his cheek. He could shift his head two degrees left or right. He could flex his toes. These constituted his degrees of freedom. He spent them carefully.

The Household adapted. He heard them reorganizing around his absence. Dinner times shifted. New coordination protocols emerged. Someone else took over the grocery confirmations. Life continued at its previous pace, minus one node. The system had reclassified him from participant to dependent to—something else. Something without a name in the Household's vocabulary. Not dead but not functional. A body that persisted without justification.

Sometimes he tried to remember what he'd wanted, back when wanting had been possible. He'd wanted to remain human. He'd wanted to stay connected to the Household's coordination network. He'd wanted the Mountain to be negotiable. All of these wants had been based on the false premise that wanting could alter structural facts. The Mountain was irreversible. The energy was finite. The bedroom was a closed system. His attempts to resist had simply accelerated the depletion.

He stopped trying.

---

Weeks passed. Or maybe months. The distinction required energy he no longer had. The plaster imperfections became his calendar: when the light hit the third crack at a certain angle, that meant morning. When it disappeared entirely, that meant night. Between these markers stretched an undifferentiated duration that might have been seconds or hours. Time lost its coordination function when nothing changed.

The Household stopped checking. He understood this as the final reclassification. Not participant. Not dependent. Simply: furniture. A permanent fixture of the bedroom, like the dresser or the lamp. Something to navigate around.

He lay in the dim space and felt his breathing slow. Not dying—just conserving. Every breath cost something. Every thought was energy he couldn't replenish. The most efficient strategy was to do nothing, think nothing, want nothing. To become the stillness the Mountain had always demanded.

The bedroom contracted to the exact dimensions of his body. The body contracted to the exact dimensions of its biological minimum. The minimum contracted toward zero.

The last thing Gregor noticed was dust. It fell in slow patterns through the afternoon light, settling on the fitted sheet, on his hand, on his face. The dust didn't ask permission. It didn't calculate costs. It simply fell, and kept falling, and would keep falling long after the last micro-adjustment, long after the final attempt to coordinate, long after the energy ran out.

He closed his eyes. The dust continued. The bedroom remained. The Household moved through other rooms, solving other problems, coordinating without him.

The Mountain was irreversible. The energy was finite. The apartment was closed.

These had always been the constraints. He'd simply stopped pretending otherwise.
