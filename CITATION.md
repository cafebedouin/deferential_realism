## Author

**cafebedouin** is a pseudonym. The author has chosen not to attach institutional credentials to this work.

This is intentional: the framework argues for evaluating constraints by their structure rather than by the authority presenting them. The same standard applies here—judge the framework by its internal coherence, empirical grounding, and practical utility, not by who wrote it.
